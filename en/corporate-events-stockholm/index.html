Why boat charter works as a corporate event in Stockholm

There is a reason why outdoor experiences in natural settings build stronger bonds than indoor activities. People relax. Conversations become different. Hierarchies flatten a little. On a boat in the archipelago, it is hard not to have real conversations with each other.

It is not about doing something fancy. It is about removing the everyday routine and giving the team a shared experience – something to refer back to. The archipelago charter in Stockholm does exactly that.

Reward day

A day for the team that has truly performed. A simple message: you have earned this.
